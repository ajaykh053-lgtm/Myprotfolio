Digital Attandance system

The Digital Attendance System is an automated solution designed to simplify attendance tracking for schools, colleges, and workplaces. Instead of relying on manual registers or paper sheets, this system uses digital technology to record, store, and analyze attendance data efficiently. Key Features Automated Attendance Logging: Uses ID cards, facial recognition, or QR codes to mark attendance instantly. Real-Time Data Access: Admins and teachers can view attendance records anytime, anywhere. Smart Notifications: Sends alerts for absentees or late arrivals via email or SMS. Data Analytics: Generates daily, weekly, and monthly reports to track attendance trends. Secure Database: Stores all records safely with encryption and role-based access. Benefits Eliminates human error in attendance marking. Saves time and improves administrative efficiency. Enhances transparency and accountability. Can be integrated with payroll or academic grading systems.
